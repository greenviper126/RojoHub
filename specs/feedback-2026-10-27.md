# Feedback 2026-10-27: final 0.22.12 test round

From the TheLaundryShift, Isoliminality and VluxyBuildingTools agent sessions, after `ensure`
(spec 015) reopened each project's place onto plugin 0.22.12. All three passed every step:
`version`; `ensure` reopened their place, came back synced, and said "done"; a second `ensure`
reopened nothing; `status` and `log` were clean; Studio's synced script matched the worktree.
What follows is everything they found odd or annoying across the whole test period. It is logged
only: nothing is fixed until Viper says go.

| # | Finding | Who | Proposed (default) |
